# Notice on Enrollment Increases for First-Year Major Courses (CSE1017 Programming Fundamentals / COM2018 Object-Oriented Programming)
 - [Important]. *The first session will be held as a regular class.* Please make sure to attend the first class. All other conditions being equal, priority for enrollment-increase requests will be given to students who attend the first class. (Exceptions may be made for special circumstances, but justification is required.)
Enrollment increases will not be granted during the pre-semester registration period. All enrollment-increase procedures will be handled only during the add/drop period after the semester begins.
 - Enrollment caps may be imposed in a given semester depending on circumstances (classroom environment, maximum allowed capacity, etc.).
 - Enrollment-increase requests may be denied in the following cases:
    - The student committed serious academic misconduct in a previous class (e.g., misconduct related to assignments or coursework).
    - The student was admitted to a previous class through an additional enrollment increase but received an F due to insufficient attendance without a valid reason (i.e., failure to attend in good faith).
